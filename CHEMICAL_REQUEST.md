Subject: Request for reagents and lab consumables for the Tech4Food 2026 final round (Team IRIS)

Respected Sir/Madam,

Team IRIS has qualified for the final round of Tech4Food 2026, to be held at PCCOE on 24 October 2026. Our project detects milk adulteration from a single photograph of a reagent strip. Iodine identifies starch; methylene blue identifies detergent.

We trained our model on 534 synthetic strip images. The final will run on real reagent strips, so we need to photograph prepared strips in at least three lighting conditions and retrain before 22 October. That is what the chemicals are for.

We request the following, in small quantities:

- Iodine solution (Lugol's or tincture, dilute): 10 mL
- Methylene blue (dilute aqueous solution): 10 mL
- Distilled water: 50 mL
- Filter paper, Whatman No. 1 or similar, 11 cm circles: 2 to 3 pieces
- Disposable Pasteur pipettes: 10
- Amber dropper bottle, 20 to 30 mL: 1
- Nitrile gloves: 4 to 6 pairs

One test uses one or two drops, so 10 mL of each reagent covers more than a hundred tests, well beyond what the preparation period and the final require. Both solutions can be dilute. If you have a concentration you prefer for a starch demonstration, we will use what you recommend.

If the lab holds SDS (sodium dodecyl sulphate), a few millilitres of it would give a more reproducible detergent reaction than dish soap. Not essential, but useful.

Two points we need your guidance on:

1. Whether these quantities may be carried to PCCOE on 24 October, or should be arranged locally on the day.
2. Whether a lab bench is available on 20 to 22 October for preparing and photographing the strips.

We will work with gloves, keep every bottle sealed and labelled, use dilute solutions only, and return whatever is left. Nothing leaves the lab without your approval.
